Terms for garment requests

Version 1 · Last updated 28 October 2026

These terms apply each time you request a garment designed in the TailorTech configurator. By ticking “I have read and accept the Terms” you confirm that you agree to them for that request.

1. A request, not a purchase

Sending a garment request does not create a purchase or any obligation to pay. We review every request and contact you to confirm the details, the final price and the delivery time. An order is only placed once you confirm that offer.

2. Prices

Prices shown in the configurator and in your cart are estimates based on the options you selected. The final price is confirmed by us before anything is made and can differ, for example because of fabric availability or special requests in your notes.

3. Made to measure

  • Each garment is cut and sewn individually from the measurements and options you provide. Please check them carefully.
  • We may contact you to confirm or adjust measurements before production starts.
  • Fabric colours can look slightly different on screen than in real life, and the 3D and drawn previews are illustrations, not exact representations.

4. Changes and cancellation

You can change or withdraw a request at any time before you confirm our offer. Because garments are made to your personal specifications, the right to withdraw from a confirmed order may not apply once production has started. We will tell you exactly when that point is reached.

5. Your data

We use your account details, measurements and garment choices to prepare and make your garment and to contact you about it. We will only send you marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and every marketing email contains a link to unsubscribe. You can also change this in your dashboard.

6. Contact

Questions about these terms or your request: tailortech@toom.design.
